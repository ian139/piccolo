# Piccolo macOS App Icon Design Brief

## Goal
Design and generate a native macOS app icon for **Piccolo**, a high-velocity photo triage app for macOS.

## Concept & Visual Metaphor
- **Theme**: Rapid photo triage, decision sorting, and focused image curation.
- **Visual Elements**:
  - A tactile stack of glossy, minimalist photo prints / slides rendered in an authentic macOS squircle icon tile.
  - Crisp, clean lighting with realistic glass, card depth, subtle drop shadow, and dynamic elevation.
  - Minimal accents hinting at the triage actions: Keep (clean emerald/leaf green accent), Pass (soft neutral slate), and Trash (subtle ruby/crimson edge).
  - Modern Apple Human Interface Guidelines aesthetic (subtle skeuomorphism + clean geometry, similar to native macOS Sonoma/Sequoia system utilities like Photos, Preview, or Keynote).
- **Format**:
  - High resolution 1024x1024 master artwork.
  - Square 1:1 aspect ratio centered on a dark/neutral backdrop or standard macOS rounded squircle plate.
  - No text, letters, or noisy watermarks.
